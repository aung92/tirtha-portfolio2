About Tirtha

I'm a Visual Identity & Brand Designer with a passion for creating meaningful design that lives between art and strategy.

With over 5 years of experience working with startups, luxury brands, and cultural institutions, I believe that great design tells a story and creates emotional connections.

My approach is research-driven and collaborative — every project begins with understanding the unique essence of the brand.
